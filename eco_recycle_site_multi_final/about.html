About the Initiative

This pilot program rewards citizens who bring recyclable materials to partner centers in Pune. Credits earned are redeemable as discounts on electricity bills. Our mission is to promote sustainability, community engagement, and a cleaner city.

Vision

Build a circular economy in Pune and reduce waste generation.

Partners

Local NGOs, municipal bodies, and certified recyclers.
